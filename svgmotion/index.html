SVGMotion

Free AI SVG animation studio — prompt a scene, edit it, export a plain .svg that stays interactive.

Most vector animation tools lock your output to their runtime — Rive needs .riv, Lottie needs lottie-web. SVGMotion is built the other way around: the export is a self-contained .svg file that renders anywhere a browser does, yet stays interactive and callable from JavaScript. Describe a scene, edit keyframes and state machines, bake physics, and ship a file that needs no runtime, no account, and no watermarks.

🎨

Three studios, one format

Vector Editor for animated scenes, Character Studio for rigged worlds, Asset Studio for OS-ready icon bundles.

📞

Callable exports

Every .svg embeds a tiny player API — player.call('launch'), setInput() — drive it from your code.

⚡

Bake-then-play physics

Bounce, roll, pendulum & more are simulated once into ordinary keyframes — no engine ships with your file.

🔒

Local-first & BYOK AI

Projects stay in your browser and it works offline. Bring your own Ollama, OpenRouter, or OpenAI key — or the in-browser WebGPU engine.

🆓

Free forever

No sign-up, no ads, no watermarks on exports. Open source under MIT.

🧩

Every gallery item is a real export — call its methods, preview it, or open it as an editable copy.
