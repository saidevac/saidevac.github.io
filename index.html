Flagship · Live web app

SVGMotion

Free AI SVG Animation Studio

Most animation tools lock your output to their runtime — Rive needs .riv, Lottie needs lottie-web. SVGMotion exports a plain, self-contained .svg that renders anywhere, yet stays interactive and callable from code.

🎨

Three studios, one format

Vector Editor for animated scenes, Character Studio for rigged worlds, Asset Studio for OS-ready icon bundles.

📞

Callable exports

Every .svg embeds a tiny player API — player.call('launch'), setInput() — drive it from your code.

⚡

Bake-then-play physics

Bounce, roll, pendulum & more are simulated once into ordinary keyframes — no engine ships with your file.

🔒

Local-first & BYOK AI

Projects stay in your browser and it works offline. Bring your own Ollama, OpenRouter, or OpenAI key — or the in-browser WebGPU engine.

🆓

Free forever

No sign-up, no ads, no watermarks on exports. Open source under MIT.

🧩

Remixable gallery

Every gallery item is a real export — call its methods, preview it, or open it as an editable copy.

Live now — no install, no account

Runs entirely in your browser. Type a prompt like “a rocket that launches on click”, edit it, export a callable .svg.

The internet is full of amazing content, but also endless distractions. Read On cuts through the noise. It's built for the discerning reader who values deep focus. Our proprietary conversion engine strips away ads, pop-ups, and extraneous elements, converting complex web pages into clean, perfectly formatted EPUB files.

  • ✓ Kindle-Optimized: Guaranteed beautiful formatting for all Kindle devices.
  • ✓ One-Tap Saving: Send articles from any browser or share menu directly to your queue.
  • ✓ Offline Access: Read your saved articles wherever you are, without an internet connection.
  • ✓ Queue Management: Organize your backlog with tags and a simple, intuitive interface.
